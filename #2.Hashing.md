# Easy

| S.No | Problem | Approach | Time Complexity | Space Complexity |
|------|---------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| 1 | Highest Occurring Element in an Array | use hashmap to count frequency, track max | O(n) | O(n) |
| 2 | Second Highest Occurring Element | freqmap , first update max and equal to case , then update second max and equal to case | O(n) | O(n) |
| 3 | Sum of Highest and Lowest Frequency | freqmap , max , min | O(n) | O(n) |
